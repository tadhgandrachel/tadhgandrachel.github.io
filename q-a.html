Q&A

What time is the ceremony?

Our ceremony begins at 2pm on Friday, 9 April 2027. Please arrive from 1pm so you have time for a drink and to find your seat. Come dressed and ready to celebrate, as room check-in is from 3pm.

What is the dress code?

Cocktail for the wedding on Friday, and casual for Day Two at The Boathouse on Saturday.

What date should I RSVP by?

Please RSVP by 1 February 2027 using your personal invitation link. If you need to change a reply you have already submitted, please contact us directly.

Is there parking available at The Ravenswood?

Yes, there is on-site parking at The Ravenswood. You can find the venue map and travel advice on our Getting there page.

What type of food will be served at the wedding?

We will share menu details closer to the day. Please include any allergies or dietary requirements in your RSVP so we can pass them on to the venue.

Am I allowed to bring a plus-one?

Your personal invitation and RSVP page show the guests invited to celebrate with us. Please RSVP for the people listed on your invitation; we are unable to accommodate additional guests.

Where can we stay for the wedding?

The Ravenswood has rooms available for our wedding night, booked using our accommodation code. Visit Places to stay for the booking link, code and nearby alternatives.

Are children invited?

Our wedding will be an adults-only celebration. Thank you for understanding — we hope you can enjoy a lovely day and evening celebrating with us.
