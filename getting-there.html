Getting there

The wedding is at The Ravenswood, Cinder Hill, Sharpthorne, West Sussex, RH19 4HY. Gatwick is the nearest airport — about 20–25 minutes by taxi.

Coming from London

Take a train to East Grinstead, Haywards Heath or Gatwick Airport, then a taxi to The Ravenswood. All three stations are an easy taxi ride from the venue, with rail connections from Clapham Junction.

Alternatively, you can take a taxi or Uber all the way from London to the venue.

Local taxis

If you are staying elsewhere or heading home after the wedding, we would recommend you book your taxi in advance. We have a late bar until 1am, with final taxi pickups at 1:30am. Please book your pickup for no later than 1:30am.

Uber is also available, but again should be booked in advance. Mobile phone reception is very limited in the area, so arrange your journey before arriving rather than relying on booking a ride at the end of the night.
