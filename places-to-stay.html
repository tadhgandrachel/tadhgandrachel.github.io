Arrival & breakfast

Check-in is from 3pm, after the ceremony and initial photos. Check-out is 10:30am, and breakfast is served from 8–10am.

The venue opens one hour before the ceremony, with the bar open for pre-ceremony drinks. There are no changing or luggage facilities before then, so please arrive dressed and ready for the day.

If the on-site rooms go, East Grinstead, Forest Row, and Sharpthorne are close and have inns, B&Bs, and small hotels. Haywards Heath is a little further and has more options, still an easy taxi to the venue.
